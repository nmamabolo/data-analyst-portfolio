# 10 — Streaming Content Analytics

## Scenario

A streaming product team wants to compare content engagement while controlling for episode length and repeated viewing sessions.

## Deliverables

- Completion rate and watch hours by genre
- Top titles within each genre
- User sessionization with a 30-minute inactivity rule
- Binge-session identification

Run [`solution.sql`](solution.sql). A view is complete when at least 90% of its title duration was watched.
