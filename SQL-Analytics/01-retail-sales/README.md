# 01 — Retail Sales Performance

## Scenario

A multi-region retailer wants a monthly performance view that explains growth and identifies the categories contributing most to revenue.

## Deliverables

- Monthly revenue and month-over-month growth
- Category contribution to total sales
- Regional sales ranking
- Average order value and unique customers

Run [`solution.sql`](solution.sql) in PostgreSQL. The final three result sets are ready to connect to a BI dashboard.
